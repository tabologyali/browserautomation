**Ballast & hedges (~15%):** GLD 7.5% (sticky inflation + tariff event),

**Cash 7.9%:** dry powder explicitly reserved for the late-July tariff
announcement. If it causes a >5% broad drawdown, deploy into the highest-
conviction names.

## How this beats SPY (the actual bet)

High active share vs the index: overweight AI infrastructure earnings
power, underweight the expensive middle of the index, plus EM/small-cap
breadth and a tariff hedge SPY doesn't have. If AI capex stalls, BRK-B,
GLD, and cash soften the hit — but make no mistake, this portfolio loses
to SPY in a sharp AI unwind. That's the risk being paid for.

## Review cadence

>15% of portfolio, thesis break, or the tariff event.
